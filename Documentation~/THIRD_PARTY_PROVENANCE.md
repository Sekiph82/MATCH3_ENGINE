## Adoption decision

Only the LibraStack SDK is the selected match-3 logic candidate. Its exact package, dependency, and per-file notice checks continue in ENG-M00-001 and ENG-M00-003. No component in this inventory is vendored by ENG-M00-009. Until a matching retained notice is present, the policy validator rejects a LibraStack or Alperen source tree. The three reference-only projects and every unidentified or unlicensed source remain absent from executable project trees.
